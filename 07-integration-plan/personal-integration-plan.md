# Personal Integration Plan

1. **Recurring Task 1:** Customer Feedback Synthesis.
   - **Expected Benefit:** Save 2 hours weekly on manual reading.
   - **Preferred Workflow:** Information Workflow extraction prompt.
   - **Risk/Verification:** Check for missed or misinterpreted complaints.
   - **Usage Frequency:** Weekly.
   - **What Success Looks Like:** Rapid identification of trending app issues before they escalate.

2. **Recurring Task 2:** Drafting Apology and Update Emails.
   - **Expected Benefit:** Consistent, empathetic, and professional communication.
   - **Preferred Workflow:** Writing Workflow 4-step process.
   - **Risk/Verification:** Fact-check refund timelines before sending.
   - **Usage Frequency:** Daily.
   - **What Success Looks Like:** Faster response turnaround time and improved CSAT scores.

3. **Recurring Task 3:** CX Improvement Planning.
   - **Expected Benefit:** Structured roadmap generation for new initiatives.
   - **Preferred Workflow:** Planning Chain (Goal → Mechanisms → Phases).
   - **Risk/Verification:** Validate team capacity with department leads.
   - **Usage Frequency:** Monthly.
   - **What Success Looks Like:** Clear, actionable steps for support teams to follow.
