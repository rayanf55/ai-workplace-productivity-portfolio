# Verification Checklist & Application

## Personal Verification Checklist
1. Identify the core data points (dates, names, metrics)[cite: 1].
2. Set accurate verification criteria against source documents or CRM[cite: 1].
3. Test AI output against trusted organizational records[cite: 1].
4. Confirm constraints and flag missing information as [Not Specified][cite: 1].
5. Document final decision and human approval[cite: 1].

## Applied Example
**Output Verified:** The AI-Generated Draft from the "Professional Writing Workflow" (Section 2).
**Application Steps:**
- **Criteria 1 (Metrics):** I verified the AI-suggested refund timeline. The AI hallucinated "we will send your money back soon". I corrected this by verifying against our SLA and updating it to the precise "3-5 business days".
- **Criteria 3 (Trusted Records):** I cross-referenced the customer's claim of a "double charge" directly in our payment gateway (Stripe) before confirming it in the email draft.
