# Professional Writing Workflow Example

## 1. AI-Generated Draft
[Draft text generated by AI based on a prompt asking to reply to a customer who was double-charged during checkout]
"Hi there, I am so sorry you got charged twice. Our app was freezing. We will send your money back soon. Let us know if you need anything else."

## 2. Fact Verification
- Verified the double charge in the payment gateway system.
- Confirmed that the refund processing time takes 3-5 business days.
- Ensured no sensitive customer data (like credit card numbers) is included in the email[cite: 1].

## 3. Tone Refinement
- Adjusted tone from overly casual to professional, empathetic, and reassuring.
- Structured the email to clearly state the action taken and the expected timeline.

## 4. Human Sign-off
- Reviewed, refined, and approved by: ريان فيصل أبو درمان (Customer Experience Analyst)[cite: 1].

## 5. Final Version
Dear Customer,

Thank you for reaching out and sharing your experience. I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience you faced with our app freezing and the resulting double charge during your checkout. 

I want to assure you that I have personally investigated this issue. We have initiated a full refund for the duplicate charge, which should reflect in your account within 3-5 business days. Our technical team is also working on resolving the app stability issue.

We deeply value your business and your patience. If you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to reply directly to this email.

Best regards,

ريان فيصل أبو درمان
Customer Experience Analyst
