# Responsible AI & Governance Analysis

## Six Practical Concepts
- **Accuracy:** Cross-checking AI-generated summaries against the original customer feedback text.
- **Accountability:** Human review ensures ultimate ownership of all published CX reports[cite: 1].
- **Confidentiality:** Zero use of real customer data, emails, or internal financial credentials[cite: 1].
- **Privacy:** Protecting personal user data by anonymizing all feedback examples[cite: 1].
- **Bias:** Ensuring complaint escalation rules apply equally regardless of customer demographic.
- **Human Oversight:** Retaining final sign-off authority on all external client emails[cite: 1].
